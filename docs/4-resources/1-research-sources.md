# Research Sources & Policy

## Research Policy

⚠️ All research MUST rely on reputable and verified sources only. Data should be sourced from established UN agencies, peer-reviewed journals, recognized international NGOs, academic institutions, and official government statistics.

This applies to:
- AI-generated blog content (Plan 6 Step 3)
- Blog sidebar "Latest Research" section
- Any statistics cited on the website
